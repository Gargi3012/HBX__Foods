# HBX FOODS — Export Quality & Technical Specifications Manual

This technical specification manual outlines the physicochemical benchmarks, microbiological limits, grading criteria, packaging engineering standards, and pre-shipment compliance protocols governing all export consignments from **HBX Foods**.

---

## 1. Compliance & Statutory Governance

All HBX Foods shipments conform to the import hygiene regulations of destination markets, including:
- **United States:** US-FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) & ASTA Cleanliness Specifications.
- **European Union:** Regulation (EC) No 1881/2006 (Maximum levels for contaminants) and Regulation (EC) No 396/2005 (MRLs of pesticides).
- **United Kingdom:** UK-FSA imported food standards.
- **Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC):** GSO 1016 / 2014 microbiological criteria for foodstuffs.
- **India (Origin):** FSSAI, Spice Board of India, APEDA, and Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage.

## 4. Packaging Engineering & Seaport Container Stuffing

### 4.1 Packaging Materials
- **Dehydrated Flakes:** 20kg 5-ply kraft paper bags with 80-micron inner food-grade Polyethylene (PE) liner, heat-sealed.
- **Seeds & Grains:** 25kg or 50kg multi-layer polypropylene (PP) woven bags with UV resistance and inner PE liner.
- **Whole Exotics (Saffron / Cardamom):** Nitrogen-flushed aluminum food-grade tins or vacuum barrier pouch packs.
- **Bulk Ground Spices:** 25kg fiber drums with double PE liner or multi-ply moisture barrier paper bags.

### 4.2 20' FCL & 40' HC Container Loading Standards
- **20' Standard Dry FCL:** 
  - Bagged Whole Seeds: 18.0 to 20.0 Metric Tons (approx. 720 to 800 bags of 25kg).
  - Dehydrated Flakes: 7.0 to 9.0 Metric Tons (due to high volumetric bulk density).
  - Mixed Consolidations: Optimized through the HBX Container Bag Engine up to 20.0 Metric Tons payload.
- **Moisture & Condensation Defense:** Every export container is lined with food-grade kraft paper and fitted with minimum 4x 1.0kg high-absorption calcium chloride container desiccants to prevent container rain during trans-oceanic transit.
- **Palletization & Fumigation:** Heat-treated wooden pallets complying with ISPM-15, certified fumigation with Methyl Bromide or Aluminium Phosphide with issued Phytosanitary and Fumigation Certificates.
