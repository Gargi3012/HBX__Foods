## 🏛️ Brand Overview

**HBX Foods** is an export-focused processor and bulk supplier of authentic Indian whole spices, optical Sortex-cleaned seeds, cryogenic cold-ground spice blends, and industrial dehydrated vegetables. Operating across prime agricultural mandis in Rajasthan (*Bikaner, Jodhpur, Raisinghnagar*), we supply commercial food processors, industrial spice blenders, and hypermarket distributors worldwide.

- **Primary Export Standard:** MOQ 20 Metric Tons / 20' FCL (or 20 LCL consolidated).
- **Compliance & Certifications:** FSSAI, Export Inspection Council, Phytosanitary Clearance, Full Certificate of Analysis (COA) per batch.
- **Global Hubs:** Mohali (India HQ) • Ontario (Canada) • London (UK) • Sharjah Media City (UAE).

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Architectural System

The HBX Foods web presence is built on the **Saffron Muse** and **Noiré** architectural luxury aesthetic:
- **Palette:** Matte Espresso (`#12100E`), Roasted Slate (`#181512`), Plaster Bone (`#F4ECE4`), Burnt Terracotta (`#8E3B29`).
- **Typography:** *Cormorant Garamond* (Royal Editorial Serif) paired with *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Clean Modern Sans).
- **Layout:** True 1-Screen Viewport Hero (`calc(100vh - 64px)`), Compact Organic Arch Frames, and an Apple-Style Glassmorphic Navigation Dock.
